    Our place

    A hidden gem, if you can call somewhere this busy hidden

    We are the little corner building where St George’s Road meets the walk down to the bay — dark walls, warm lamps, pale pink everywhere, and a window seat worth arriving early for.

    Mornings are pancakes, coffee and dogs asleep under tables. Lunchtimes are burgers, buttermilk chicken and whatever the specials board says. Thursday to Saturday the lights drop, the candles come out and the kitchen cooks a proper evening menu.

    Everything is made to order by a small team who mostly live within walking distance. If you are not sure what to have, ask — recommending things is the part of the job they enjoy most.

    “A hidden gem on the seafront. Lovely décor, lively room, and they are always good at recommending things.” Local food blog

    Before you come

    The things people ring up to ask

    Do I need to book?

    Not for a coffee or a couple of people at breakfast — walk in and we’ll find you a seat. For a table of four or more, weekend brunch, or any evening Thursday to Saturday, ring 0191 252 3442 and we’ll hold one for you.

    When does breakfast stop being served?

    Breakfast runs from 8am until 12pm every day, then the main menu takes over until 4:30pm. The banner at the top of this site counts down to the changeover, and warns you a full hour before, so you always know whether you can still get a pancake stack.

    Are you open in the evenings?

    Thursday, Friday and Saturday. The kitchen serves the evening menu from 5pm until last orders at 8:30pm, and the room stays open until 9pm. Sunday to Wednesday we close at 5pm.

    Can I bring my dog?

    Please do. We’re dog friendly all day, every day, inside and out, with water bowls by the door.

    Is there anything for vegans and coeliacs?

    Yes to both. Vegan and vegetarian dishes are marked on every menu, and a good number of plates are gluten free or can be made so — look for the GF and GFa marks. Tell your server about any allergy when you order: our kitchen is small and handles nuts, gluten and dairy, so we’ll talk you through what is safe.

    Do you do takeaway and delivery?

    Both. Ring to order a collection, or find us on Uber Eats and Deliveroo for delivery around Cullercoats, Whitley Bay and Tynemouth.

    Where can I park?

    There is free on-street parking on St George’s Road and the surrounding terraces, plus the seafront car parks a couple of minutes down the hill. On a sunny weekend, the Metro is honestly quicker — Cullercoats station is a four-minute walk away.

    Can you cater a party or a wake?

    We can. Buffets, afternoon teas and private daytime hire are all possible with a bit of notice — give us a call and we’ll put something together with you.
