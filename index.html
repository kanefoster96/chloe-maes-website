    Our place

    Light, warm, and covered in flowers

    We are the corner building where St George’s Road meets the walk down to the bay. Marble tables, blush velvet chairs, black pendant lamps and a wall of flowers behind the banquette.

    Mornings are pancakes, coffee and dogs asleep under tables. Lunchtimes are bowls, burgers and whatever the specials board says. Thursday to Saturday the lights drop and the kitchen cooks a proper evening menu.

    “A hidden gem on the seafront. Lovely décor, lively room, and they are always good at recommending things.” Local food blog

    Before you come

    Good to know

    Do I need to book?

    Not for a coffee or a couple of people at breakfast. For four or more, weekend brunch, or any evening Thursday to Saturday, ring 0191 252 3442 and we’ll hold a table.

    When does breakfast stop?

    Breakfast runs 8am to 12pm every day, then the main menu takes over until 4:30pm. The bar at the top of this site counts down to the changeover and warns you a full hour before.

    Are you open in the evenings?

    Thursday, Friday and Saturday, 5pm until last orders at 8:30pm. Sunday to Wednesday we close at 5pm.

    Can I bring my dog?

    Please do — dog friendly all day, every day, with water bowls by the door.

    Vegan, vegetarian or coeliac?

    All catered for, and marked on every menu. Tell your server about any allergy when you order — our kitchen is small and handles nuts, gluten and dairy, so we’ll talk you through what is safe.

    Takeaway and delivery?

    Both. Ring to order a collection, or find us on Uber Eats and Deliveroo around Cullercoats, Whitley Bay and Tynemouth.
