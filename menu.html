Allergies and intolerances. Our kitchen is small and handles nuts, gluten, dairy, eggs, fish and sesame, so we cannot promise any dish is completely free from traces. Please tell your server before you order and we will talk you through what we can do. Full allergen information is available on request, and dishes marked GFa or VEa can be adapted.

Specials change through the week and are not listed here — ask at the counter or check @chloemaes_cullercoats. Card and cash both welcome. A discretionary service charge is never added.
